01 — The legend

Long before engines, a hero rode a horse called Rakhsh. The name meant lightning. It never tired, it never lost, and it could outrun the night itself.

We spent six years building the next one.

02 — The machine

Drawn as one line. Cut from one block.

000°

01 / Light bar

One line of light.

A single 4,000-lumen bar is the headlight, the indicator and the signature. Switched off, it disappears into the body.

02 / Monocoque

One piece of metal.

Frame, tank and tail are forged from a single block of aluminium. Nothing bolted on, nothing to rattle loose.

03 / Hub motor

The engine is the wheel.

62 kW of copper-wound torque lives inside the rear hub. No chain, no belt, no oil, no noise.

04 / Swingarm

Nothing in the way.

A single-sided swingarm leaves the rear wheel bare, so the right side of the bike is all wheel and light.
